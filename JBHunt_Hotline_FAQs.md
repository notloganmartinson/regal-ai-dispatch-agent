# J.B. Hunt Emergency Dispatch & Hotline FAQs

This knowledge base serves as the definitive operational manual for the automated dispatch voice agent. It provides immediate, contextual guidance to drivers experiencing on-the-road disruptions, mechanical failures, or logistics crises.

---

## 1. Compensation & Detention Pay

### Q: Do I get paid if my truck breaks down or I am delayed by traffic?
**A:** Yes. J.B. Hunt provides **Mechanical Breakdown Pay** and **Rail Delay Pay** for active company drivers. While exact rates may vary slightly depending on your specific account or route, drivers typically earn between $20.00 and $23.00 per hour during mechanical breakdowns, major road delay events, or while waiting on a replacement power unit.

### Q: What happens if I arrive at the new warehouse reroute destination and they make me wait to unload?
**A:** We value your time. If you are held at a receiver or shipping dock, J.B. Hunt provides **Detention Pay**. Active drivers earn between $20.00 and $33.00 per hour for detention time. This compensation typically kicks in automatically after the first hour of waiting past your scheduled appointment time at any live loading/unloading stop.

## 2. Roadside Assistance & Technical Support

### Q: Does J.B. Hunt provide roadside assistance if my truck is disabled?
**A:** Yes. If your tractor or trailer is disabled due to a mechanical breakdown anywhere on the road, J.B. Hunt coordinates emergency roadside services 24/7/365. This includes dispatching mobile mechanics, organizing commercial towing services to a certified repair facility, and sending emergency fluid deliveries.

### Q: What do I do if the J.B. Hunt DRIVE app or Carrier 360 app freezes while trying to view my new route?
**A:** If you experience technical difficulties with the **J.B. Hunt DRIVE app** while on the road, contact our 24/7 Technical Service Desk directly at **800-723-0421**. For independent contractors experiencing issues with the **Carrier 360 app**, please reach out to our dedicated support line at **833-211-4392** during standard operational business hours.

## 3. Transit Expenses & Fueling

### Q: Does J.B. Hunt pay for toll roads on my new detour or reroute path?
**A:** Yes. All company-issued trucks are equipped with pre-activated **Bestpass / PrePass transponders**, which automatically cover approved toll systems across the country. In the rare event that you encounter a toll booth that does not accept the electronic transponder, pay the toll using personal funds, save the physical receipt, and submit it directly through the J.B. Hunt DRIVE app under "Reimbursements" for a full payout on your next check.

### Q: Where am I allowed to fuel up my truck during an adjusted or crisis reroute?
**A:** To optimize fuel efficiency and corporate compliance, you must fuel exclusively at our preferred partner network locations using your issued J.B. Hunt Comdata fuel card. These networks include **Pilot Flying J, Love's Travel Stops, and TA/Petro**. The J.B. Hunt DRIVE app will automatically update and display the optimized fuel stops along your adjusted route.

## 4. Compliance, Safety & Accidents

### Q: How do I handle my Electronic Logging Device (ELD) hours of service if this crisis puts me over my daily limit?
**A:** Safety and Department of Transportation (DOT) compliance are strictly non-negotiable. If an unforeseen severe traffic delay or mechanical breakdown threatens to violate your 11-hour driving limit or 14-hour on-duty limit, utilize the official **"Adverse Driving Conditions"** exception in your ELD interface if applicable. If you run out of legal hours completely, you must pull over safely at the nearest legal parking spot (such as a truck stop or rest area) and notify dispatch immediately so that we can formally adjust the warehouse delivery window.

### Q: What should I do if I am involved in a minor accident or fender-bender while executing this reroute?
**A:** 
1. **Safety First:** Ensure you are entirely unhurt, move to a safe zone if possible, and dial 911 immediately if there are any physical injuries.
2. **Report Instantly:** Notify the J.B. Hunt Safety and Claims Hotline immediately at **800-723-0422**. 
3. **Protect Yourself:** Do not sign any third-party statements or admit fault at the scene. 
4. **Document:** Take clear, comprehensive photos of all vehicles involved, visible property damage, road conditions, and the official police report number. Submit these digital files through the "Safety" tab in your J.B. Hunt DRIVE app as soon as the scene is secure.
